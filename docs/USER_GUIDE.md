## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1 Creating an Account

1. Open KrishiSaathi at **https://krishisaathi-ai-hackathon.streamlit.app/**
2. Click **"Sign Up"** on the login page
3. Enter your:
   - **Full Name**
   - **Email Address**
   - **Password** (minimum 6 characters)
   - **Location** (your village/district)
   - **Phone Number** (optional)
4. Click **"Create Account"**
5. Check your email for a confirmation link (if enabled)

### 1.2 Logging In

1. Open the app
2. Enter your **email** and **password**
3. Click **"Sign In"**
4. You'll be taken to the main chat page

### 1.3 Navigation

The **sidebar** (left panel) provides:

| Icon | Page | What It Does |
|------|------|-------------|
| 💬 | **Chat** | General AI farming assistant |
| 🌱 | **Crop Doctor** | Disease detection from photos |
| 💰 | **Market Prices** | Mandi prices and trends |
| 🏛️ | **Government Schemes** | Scheme eligibility and guidance |
| 🌤️ | **Weather** | Location-based forecasts |
| 🧪 | **Soil Expert** | Soil analysis and fertilizer advice |

## 2. Home — Chat with KrishiSaathi

The main chat page works like messaging a farming expert.

### How to Use

1. Type your question in the text box at the bottom
2. Press **Enter** or click **Send**
3. KrishiSaathi will think for a few seconds, then respond
4. You can ask follow-up questions — the AI remembers your conversation

### Example Questions

| Topic | Sample Question |
|-------|----------------|
| Crop disease | "My tomato leaves are turning yellow with brown spots. What should I do?" |
| Market | "What is the current price of rice in Hyderabad mandi?" |
| Scheme | "Am I eligible for PM-KISAN? I have 2 acres in Telangana." |
| Weather | "Will it rain in Nalgonda this week?" |
| Soil | "My soil pH is 5.5. Which fertilizer should I use for cotton?" |
| General | "What is the best time to sow groundnut in Telangana?" |

### Smart Routing

KrishiSaathi automatically understands your question type and routes it to the right specialist:

- Disease questions → **Crop Doctor Agent**
- Price questions → **Market Intelligence Agent**
- Scheme questions → **Government Scheme Advisor**
- Weather questions → **Weather Prophet Agent**
- Soil questions → **Soil Expert Agent**

### Memory

KrishiSaathi remembers important details you share:
- Your crops, location, and farm size
- Past problems and treatments
- Your preferences

This makes future conversations more personalized.

## 3. Crop Doctor

### What It Does

Upload a photo of a diseased plant and get an instant diagnosis with treatment recommendations.

### How to Use

1. Navigate to **🌱 Crop Doctor** from the sidebar
2. **Upload an image** of the affected plant (leaf, fruit, stem)
3. Optionally describe the symptoms in the text box
4. Click **"Diagnose"**
5. KrishiSaathi will:
   - Identify the disease
   - Explain the cause
   - Recommend treatment (organic + chemical options)
   - Estimate yield impact
   - Suggest prevention measures

### Tips for Best Photos

- Take photos in **natural daylight**
- Focus on the **affected area** (close-up)
- Include both **healthy and diseased** parts if possible
- Use the **rear camera** for better quality

### Supported Conditions

- Bacterial, fungal, and viral diseases
- Pest damage identification
- Nutrient deficiency symptoms (yellowing, wilting, curling)
- Over 150 crop diseases across major Indian crops

## 4. Market Prices

### What It Does

Check current mandi prices, compare across markets, and get selling advice.

### How to Use

1. Navigate to **💰 Market Prices** from the sidebar
2. Select or type your **crop** (e.g., Tomato, Rice, Cotton)

   - Current prices (min/max/modal)

Data covers major Agricultural Produce Market Committees (APMCs) across Telangana and India, including:
- Hyderabad
- Warangal
- Nizamabad
- Karimnagar
- And more across India

Discover government schemes you're eligible for and get step-by-step application help.

## 6. Weather

### What It Does

Get location-specific weather forecasts with farming-specific advisories.

### How to Use

1. Navigate to **🌤️ Weather** from the sidebar
2. Enter your **city or village name**
3. View:
   - **Current weather**: Temperature, humidity, wind, conditions
   - **5-day forecast**: Day-by-day outlook
   - **Farming advisory**: Should you irrigate? Is it safe to spray? When to harvest?

### Advisory Examples

| Condition | Advisory |
|-----------|----------|
| Rain expected | "Postpone spraying pesticides. Ensure field drainage." |
| Heat wave | "Irrigate in early morning. Apply mulch to retain moisture." |
| High humidity | "Watch for fungal infections. Increase plant spacing." |
| Frost warning | "Cover nursery beds. Avoid sowing tender crops." |

## 7. Soil Expert

### What It Does

Analyze your soil conditions and get personalized fertilizer and crop recommendations.

### How to Use

1. Navigate to **🧪 Soil Expert** from the sidebar
2. Share your soil details:
   - Soil type (Black, Red, Alluvial, Sandy, etc.)
   - Soil Health Card results (pH, N, P, K values)
   - Current crop or planned crop
3. Get recommendations for:
   - **Fertilizer plan** (quantities per acre)
   - **Organic alternatives**
   - **Crop rotation** suggestions
   - **Soil amendment** advice (lime, gypsum, etc.)

## 8. Tips for Best Results

### Do's ✅

- **Be specific**: "My 2-acre cotton field in Nalgonda has yellow leaves" works better than "crop problem"
- **Mention your location**: Advice varies by region, soil, and climate
- **Share your crop**: Different crops need different treatments
- **Upload clear photos**: For crop disease diagnosis
- **Ask follow-ups**: The AI remembers your conversation context

### Don'ts ❌

- Don't ask vague questions like "help me"
- Don't upload blurry or dark photos
- Don't expect medical/veterinary advice (it's for crops only)
- Don't share passwords or sensitive financial information

## 10. FAQ

### General

**Q: Is KrishiSaathi free?**
A: Yes, KrishiSaathi is completely free to use.

**Q: Do I need to install an app?**
A: No. KrishiSaathi runs in your web browser at the URL above.

**Q: Does it work on mobile?**
A: Yes. The interface is responsive and works on phones, tablets, and computers.

### Account

**Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?**
A: Click the **"Reset Password"** tab on the login page, enter your email address, and click **"Send Reset Link"**. You'll receive an email with a password reset link. Click the link, and you'll be taken to a secure page where you can set a new password. After successfully updating your password, you can sign in with your new credentials.

**Q: The password reset link isn't working. What should I do?**
A: Password reset links expire after a certain time. If your link has expired or isn't working, request a new password reset link from the login page. Make sure to click the link soon after receiving the email.

**Q: Can I change my language preference?**
A: Yes. Use the language dropdown in the sidebar (left panel).

### AI Responses

**Q: How accurate are the disease diagnoses?**
A: The AI uses a knowledge base of 150+ crop diseases and Gemini Vision for image analysis. While highly accurate, always verify critical diagnoses with a local agricultural extension officer.

**Q: Where does the market price data come from?**
A: From our curated database of APMC/mandi prices updated regularly, plus RAG-enhanced knowledge of market trends.

**Q: Does KrishiSaathi remember my previous conversations?**
A: Yes. Your chat history is saved and the AI remembers important details about your farm, crops, and location for personalized advice.

### Privacy

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: Yes. We use Supabase with Row-Level Security — only you can access your data. See our [Security documentation](SECURITY.md) for details.

**Q: Can other users see my conversations?**
A: No. Your chat history and memories are private and only accessible to you (and system admins for support purposes).
